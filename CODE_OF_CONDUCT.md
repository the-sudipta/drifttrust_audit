# Code of Conduct

This project follows a simple academic collaboration standard:

- be respectful,
- critique ideas and evidence, not people,
- document assumptions,
- avoid overstating experimental results,
- welcome students and early-career researchers,
- keep discussions professional and constructive.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory
language, and intentionally misleading research claims.

Project maintainers may remove comments, issues, or contributions that violate
these expectations.
