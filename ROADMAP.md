# Roadmap

## Phase 1: Runnable Research Prototype

- Synthetic telemetry generation.
- Temporal trust scoring.
- Drift detection and adaptation.
- AJR generation.
- ECI computation.
- ISO/IEC 27001 policy checks.
- Baseline comparison plots.
- Interactive one-page research showcase.

Status: implemented.

## Phase 2: Paper-Grade ML Backends

- PyTorch LSTM/GRU trust scorer.
- SHAP DeepExplainer or equivalent attribution backend.
- River ADWIN online drift detector.
- Multi-seed experiment runner.
- Configurable experiment registry.

## Phase 3: Research Validation

- Statistical reporting over repeated runs.
- Ablation studies for adaptation, AJR, ECI, and policy checks.
- Sensitivity analysis for ECI threshold.
- Comparison against RBAC, ABAC, static threshold, and non-governed DriftTrust.

## Phase 4: Publication and PhD Agenda

- Manuscript-ready figures and tables.
- Formal AJR schema.
- Human-in-the-loop review workflow.
- Governance mapping to ISO/IEC 27001, NIST SP 800-207, and SOC 2.
- Realistic enterprise-log adaptation case study.
- Funding proposal and PhD research plan.
